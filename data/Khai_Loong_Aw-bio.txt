Khai is a Computer Science PhD candidate at Stanford University. He is interested in building vision-language world models capable of tackling the complex tasks that humans perform. His research targets several big challenges in vision and AI broadly. These include zero-shot task performance without labels, data efficiency, and the integration of visual world models with language and other modalities. Before joining Stanford, he explored ways to train AI language models to align them with language-processing mechanisms in the human brain.
